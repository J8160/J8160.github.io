## A heading inside a piece

To quote a passage, start the line with a greater-than sign:

> "Hope" is the thing with feathers,
> as Emily Dickinson wrote.

To keep the line breaks of a poem exactly as you wrote them, wrap it in a `verse` block:

<pre class="verse">"Hope" is the thing with feathers -
That perches in the soul -
And sings the tune without the words -
And never stops - at all -
</pre>

The poem above is by Emily Dickinson and is in the public domain.

[^1]: Footnotes collect at the bottom of the page automatically, which is useful for citations.
